# MDR.Care Privacy Policy

Effective date: [2026/11/20]

Version: v1.0-PP

Region: Worldwide

Audience: Adults aged 18 or over only

The App does not connect to our servers, and we have no server that receives your data. Usage records from your Beauty Device go over Bluetooth to your phone, only so they can be shown to you. What you enter in the App stays on your phone.

There is no account, no cloud sync and no push notification. The App goes online only to show you the latest Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and to check for and download Beauty Device firmware updates. It connects to GitHub to do this and sends no personal data of yours. We do not collect, process or use any of your personal data through the App.

## 1. What This Policy Covers

1.1 MicroDoctor Biomedical Corporation (“we”, “us” or the “Company”) developed MDR.Care (the “App”) as the companion app for our beauty device (the “Beauty Device”). This Policy applies to all users worldwide, wherever you are.

1.2 You use the App to connect to the Beauty Device over Bluetooth and control it (for example, switching modes and adjusting intensity), to see its battery level and usage records, and to update its firmware. We do not collect, process or use your personal data through the App, and we have no server that could receive it. Section 5 explains when the App goes online.

1.3 In this Policy, “personal data” means information that identifies you directly or indirectly. It includes whatever the law where you live calls “personal information”, “personal data” or something similar. If the law where you live requires a higher level of protection, that law applies (see Section 12).

## 2. We Do Not Collect Any Personal Data

2.1 Apart from the limited situations in Section 6, which have nothing to do with how the App works, we do not:

(a) receive or store the name, email address or optional avatar you enter in the App;

(b) receive or store Beauty Device usage records (the date and length of use of each mode);

(c) receive or store the custom name you give your Beauty Device;

(d) receive or store your Beauty Device serial number or device identifier;

(e) ask you to create an account or give us a phone number (giving a name and email address is your choice, and once you enter them they stay on your phone);

(f) track what you do in the App or get your location; or

(g) sell, share or give any data to advertisers, brands, distributors or data brokers.

2.2 The App has no advertising SDK, no cross-app tracking technology, no cookies or similar tracking technology, and no push notifications. It also has nothing that sends data to us. The App does not make automated decisions or build profiles of you.

## 3. How Data Works on Your Phone and Beauty Device

3.1 Where data comes from: The Beauty Device sends usage records, battery level and firmware version to your phone over Bluetooth. You type in your name and email address yourself. You pick your avatar from your phone’s photo library, and the App receives only the picture you pick and saves a copy of it. The App does not use the camera.

3.2 Analysis: None. The App does not photograph, measure or analyze your skin. It only turns the usage records from the Beauty Device into charts.

3.3 Where data is stored: Usage records are stored on the Beauty Device. The App reads and shows them each time it connects, and does not keep them once you disconnect. Your name, email address, avatar, language setting and Beauty Device custom name (together with that device’s Bluetooth identifier) are stored in the App’s own storage area on your phone (the app sandbox). Your operating system keeps this area separate, so other apps cannot read it.

3.4 Uploads: None. The App cannot upload this data to us or to any third party.

3.5 Deleting data: You can edit or remove your name, email address and avatar on the App’s profile page. When you uninstall the App, your operating system deletes all the data stored on your phone. Usage records are stored on the Beauty Device. The App cannot delete them one by one, and uninstalling the App does not erase them. To erase them, reset the Beauty Device as the product manual explains.

3.6 Phone permissions: Your phone may ask you to allow things like Bluetooth, nearby devices or photo access. The App uses them only to connect to the Beauty Device and to pick an avatar. Some phone systems ask for location permission together with Bluetooth scanning. Even then, the App does not get or store your location. You can take back any permission at any time in your phone settings, but the related features may then stop working.

3.7 Check it yourself: Apart from showing the latest Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and checking for and downloading firmware updates, the App works without an internet connection as long as Bluetooth is on. You can use this to verify what we say here.

## 4. Backups and Your Own Cloud Storage

4.1 If you have iCloud Backup, Google Backup or a similar feature turned on, the App’s data may be copied to your own cloud storage along with your phone backup. That backup is between you and Apple or Google, is covered by their privacy policies, and is not accessible to us. You can change this in your phone’s backup settings.

## 5. Connections: Bluetooth and the Internet

5.1 The Beauty Device pairs directly with your phone over Bluetooth, and usage records travel only between those two devices. The Beauty Device itself cannot connect to the internet.

5.2 The App goes online in only two situations. First, after it connects to the Beauty Device and when you open the firmware update page, it asks GitHub for the latest firmware version, and when you choose to update, it downloads the firmware file and installs it on the Beauty Device over Bluetooth. Second, when you open the Terms of Service or Privacy Policy on the settings page, it gets the latest version from GitHub, and if it cannot connect, it shows the version built into the App.

5.3 These connections send only the request needed to get the file. They do not include your name, email address, avatar, usage records or Beauty Device identifier. So no data is sent to us while you use the Beauty Device and the App. GitHub is an independent third party, and its servers may be outside the country where you live (see Section 6).

## 6. The Only Exceptions

6.1 The situations below are not us collecting your data through the App, but we list them here so you have the full picture.

6.2 App store statistics: Apple and Google give us summary reports about the App, such as how many times it was downloaded and how often it crashed. These reports do not identify any individual. The stores collect this information under their own privacy policies, and we cannot learn who you are or see anything on your phone from them. The App itself has no crash-reporting or error-reporting feature.

6.3 GitHub connection records: GitHub serves the connections described in Section 5. Like any website, it receives connection details such as your IP address and handles them under its own privacy policy. We can see only the total number of times a firmware file has been downloaded. We cannot get these connection records or work out who you are from them.

6.4 When you contact us: If you contact us by email or any other way, we receive the contact details and message you send. This has nothing to do with how the App works. You start it yourself, and Section 9 explains how we handle it.

6.5 Legal requirements: If a court or authority asks us for information through a legal process, we will cooperate as the law requires, but only for the customer-service records described in Section 9. We cannot provide data we have never had, including your usage records, what you enter in the App and your Beauty Device serial number.

## 7. What Usage Records Are

7.1 The usage records in the App show the date and length of use of each Beauty Device mode. They are for your own reference only. The App does not analyze your skin or give skincare advice. Usage records are not a medical diagnosis or treatment recommendation and are not the output of a medical device. Please use the Beauty Device as the product manual and safety warnings say. If you are worried about a skin condition, please see a physician.

7.2 Usage records exist only on the Beauty Device and are shown on your phone, so we cannot see, review or correct them. If you think a record is wrong, please get in touch.

## 8. Age Limit

8.1 The App and the Beauty Device are only for adults. You must be at least 18 years old and an adult under the law where you live, whichever age is higher. This limit is for product safety reasons, and the App does not ask for your date of birth or check your age.

8.2 The App is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we find that a minor has given us personal data in a message described in Section 9, we will delete it.

8.3 If a parent or guardian finds that a minor is using the App, please uninstall it from that phone, which deletes the data stored on it. Usage records on the Beauty Device need to be erased separately by resetting the device as the product manual explains.

## 9. How We Handle Customer-Service Records

9.1 What this covers: The only personal data we may hold is what you give us when you contact us by email or any other way: your name, email address or other contact details, and your message (“Customer-Service Records”). Please do not include sensitive personal data in your message unless it is needed to answer your question.

9.2 Why we use it, and on what legal basis: We use Customer-Service Records only to answer your question, deal with complaints and protect our legal interests. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to your message. Where the law requires consent as the basis, Section 9.7 applies.

9.3 How long we keep it: We delete the records once your matter has been dealt with. The exceptions are where the law requires us to keep them longer, or where we need to keep them to make, exercise or defend a legal claim. In those cases we keep them until that need ends.

9.4 Who receives it: Only we do, plus service providers that run email and IT systems for us, follow our instructions and must keep the data confidential. We hand records over when a court or authority requires it through a legal process. We never sell or share Customer-Service Records.

9.5 Where it is processed: We process Customer-Service Records in Taiwan, where we are based, and wherever the service providers mentioned above are located. By sending information directly to us, you acknowledge that it will be processed in Taiwan.

9.6 Whether you have to provide it: Whether to contact us is entirely up to you. If you do not give us contact details, we cannot reply, but you can still use the App and the Beauty Device as normal.

9.7 Consent: Where the law requires your consent for processing, or for processing in another country, you give that consent when you choose to contact us after reading this Policy. This covers the processing described in this Section, including processing in Taiwan. You can withdraw your consent at any time by email. Withdrawing does not affect processing that already took place, and does not affect your use of the App or the Beauty Device.

## 10. Keeping Data Secure

10.1 We protect Customer-Service Records with reasonable organizational and technical measures, such as access controls and encryption in transit. The security of the data on your phone depends on how well you protect your phone, so please set a screen lock and keep your operating system up to date.

10.2 If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as applicable law requires.

## 11. Your Rights

11.1 Many countries give you rights such as access, inspection, copies, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. These rights are meant for personal data that an organization holds about you. For data on your phone and Beauty Device, you are the only holder. You can view, edit or delete it yourself as Section 3 explains, without asking us, and we have no way of doing it for you.

11.2 For the Customer-Service Records in Section 9, you can use these rights under applicable law: access or inspection, a copy, adding to or correcting your data, deletion, stopping or restricting processing, objecting to processing and data portability. You can also withdraw your consent. Please email us at the address in Section 15. To protect you, we may ask you to confirm who you are, but only with the minimum information we need to be sure you sent the message.

11.3 We reply within the time that applicable law sets. If the law sets no time, we reply within 30 days, and where the law allows we may extend this and tell you why. We may refuse a request that is clearly unfounded or excessive, or charge the necessary cost, where the law allows.

11.4 You cannot give up these rights in advance, and no special agreement can limit them. We will not treat you differently or give you a worse service because you use your rights.

11.5 You have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.
